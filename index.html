Creating Healthier Communities Through Care, Education & Human Rights

Who we are

A community-led organization built on trust

For years, Heart to Heart Lanka has worked alongside men who have sex with men, male sex workers, transgender people, and people living with HIV — providing sexual health education, confidential counseling, and a strong voice for human rights across Sri Lanka. Our work is led by the community, for the community.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To improve the health, wellbeing, and human rights of marginalized communities in Sri Lanka through accessible healthcare, education, counseling, and advocacy — delivered with dignity and without judgment.

Our Vision

A Sri Lanka where every person, regardless of gender identity, sexual orientation, or health status, can live safely, access the care they need, and participate fully and equally in society.

What We Focus On

Our Focus Areas

Six interconnected areas of work that support the whole person, not just a single issue.

HIV Prevention

Testing referrals, prevention education, and support delivered confidentially and without stigma.

Sexual Health Education

Accurate, practical information that helps people make informed decisions about their health.

Community Outreach

Meeting people where they are, building trust through peer-led engagement across the country.

Human Rights Advocacy

Standing up for equal treatment, legal protection, and dignity for every community we serve.

Counseling & Support

One-on-one and group counseling in a private, judgment-free space, led by trained peer counselors.

Capacity Building

Training peer educators and community leaders to sustain this work from within the community.
