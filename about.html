About Heart to Heart Lanka

Community-Led. Rights-Based. Built on Trust.

We're a Sri Lankan NGO working alongside marginalized communities to protect health, dignity, and human rights — every day, without judgment.

Our Story

Founded by the community, for the community

Heart to Heart Lanka began as a small, peer-led effort to reach communities who had nowhere safe to turn for sexual health information, testing, and support. Over time, that effort grew into a structured organization — but the core stayed the same: services designed and delivered by people who understand the community's lived reality.

Today, we work across counseling, education, outreach, and advocacy, always guided by the people we serve.

Mission

To improve the health, wellbeing, and human rights of marginalized communities in Sri Lanka through accessible healthcare, education, counseling, and advocacy.

Vision

A Sri Lanka where every person can live safely, access care without fear, and participate fully and equally in society.

What Guides Us

Our Core Values

Confidentiality

Privacy is never optional — it's the foundation of everything we do.

Respect

Every person who comes to us is treated with dignity, without exception.

Equity

We work to close the gap in access to health and justice, not widen it.

Community-Led

Decisions are shaped by the people our programs are built to serve.

Who We Serve

Standing with communities too often left out

Our programs are built around men who have sex with men (MSM), male sex workers, transgender individuals, and people living with HIV — communities that frequently face barriers to safe, respectful healthcare and legal protection.

We meet people exactly where they are, with services shaped around their real needs rather than assumptions.

Our Programs

How We Deliver Our Mission

HIV Prevention & Testing

Confidential referrals and prevention education tailored to real-world needs.

Counseling & Support

Private one-on-one and group sessions led by trained peer counselors.

Advocacy & Rights

Pushing for policy change and legal protection alongside the community.

Why Our Work Matters

Without safe, judgment-free spaces, many people delay care, hide their health status, or face discrimination when seeking help. Our work closes that gap — one relationship, one conversation, and one program at a time.
